A short list, with the reason attached.

Berkeley work is split across a course site, a Canvas page, and an inbox that also has the club newsletter. Pulse sorts that down to the week.

  • Transparent A kept email names the sender and the reason. A due date names bCourses or the course site. Nothing is sorted in secret.
  • Fast Nothing runs until you press Scan. Live dates refresh. A syllabus is parsed once, then cached.
  • Sorted Assignments, a public course calendar, and the mail that matters land on the same week. The rest stays out.
